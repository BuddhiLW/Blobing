# Bussiness ideas

## Smart listener system - for public-facing workers

While workers are attending customers, they have a device listening and making RAG-informed analysis.

If they go out of line of what's in contract etc, they receive alarms/notifications.

And, they can ask the AI to give feedback if what they told is accurate or not.
